# Introducing Ledgerline 2.0

In today's fast-paced financial landscape, teams need tools that keep up. That's why we're thrilled to announce Ledgerline 2.0 — a seamless, robust, and comprehensive platform for reconciliation.

## Why it matters

It's not just about speed, but about trust. Studies show that 73.6% of finance teams spend more than half their week on manual reconciliation. Ledgerline 2.0 changes that.

**Why it matters:** Reconciliation errors cost money. Moreover, they erode confidence in the numbers.

## What's new

**What's new:** We've completely reimagined the matching engine. The result? Matches that are faster, smarter, and more accurate.

- Faster imports
- Smarter matching
- Better reports

Acme Corp's CFO, Dana Ortiz, called it "a game-changer for our close process."

## Pricing

**Pricing:** Ledgerline 2.0 offers a Starter plan and a Pro plan. Starter is affordable and easy to use. Pro is powerful and flexible. Both are great options depending on your needs.

## Looking ahead

Ultimately, Ledgerline 2.0 is a testament to our team's commitment to excellence. We can't wait to see what you build. Let's embark on this journey together!
